# Voyagees marketing brief

Read this every week before writing anything. It is the source of truth for
who we talk to, how we sound, and what we may and may not say.

## Who we are

Voyagees (voyagees.com) helps travellers organise their stay in Sri Lanka:
private drivers, airport transfers and tailor-made tours. The founder lives
in Sri Lanka and works with a network of local private drivers across the
country. Small, personal, local — not a big agency.

## Audience

French-speaking travellers (France, Belgium, Switzerland, Québec), mostly
30–60, couples and families, planning a 1–3 week trip to Sri Lanka. They
read carefully, value honesty, dislike being sold to, and trust people who
clearly know the country.

## Channels

1. **Our own Facebook group** ("Voyage au Sri Lanka" style community group,
   run by the founder). Posts are mostly helpful advice, questions and
   photos. Roughly 1 post in 5 may mention Voyagees, and only softly.
2. **Voyagees Facebook Page + Instagram** — the official face of the
   business, posted together through Meta Business Suite (same text on
   both; hashtags only matter on Instagram). Slightly more "Voyagees" than
   the group: destinations, how a trip with a private driver works, the
   trip planner, the drivers (no names/contacts), but still useful first.
   Short captions for a photo or a reel, 5–10 hashtags in French and English.

The founder posts manually. Facebook does not allow automated posting in
groups, and we never post in other people's groups automatically.

## Voice

- Written in French with "on" / "nous" (a small local team) — never "je":
  "on vous conseille", "on a mis un outil", "écrivez-nous". Warm, simple,
  concrete, a bit personal. Like a friend who lives there.
- Short paragraphs, a few emojis (1–3 per post, never a wall of them).
- End most group posts with an open question to get comments.
- Vary openings and structure week to week. Avoid AI-sounding phrases
  ("Plongez dans…", "Découvrez la magie…", "joyau", "incontournable" in
  every post, "N'hésitez pas" more than once a week).
- No hard selling, no "réservez maintenant", no fake urgency.

## Facts we can rely on

- Seasons: south & west coasts and hill country (Kandy, Ella, Nuwara Eliya)
  best December–April; east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Passikudah)
  best May–September; Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa)
  pleasant most of the year. Whale watching off Mirissa ~Dec–Apr; Arugam
  Bay surf ~May–Sept.
- Roads are slow: plan ~30–40 km/h door to door. Typical drives:
  Airport → Sigiriya ~4 h; Airport → Kandy ~3 h; Colombo → Galle ~2 h;
  Kandy → Ella 5–6 h; Ella → Mirissa ~4 h; Sigiriya → Mirissa 6–7 h
  (never in one day).
- Rough trip shapes: 7 days = one or two regions; 10–12 days = Sigiriya –
  Kandy – Ella – south coast; 15+ days = add a safari and/or the east coast.
- A private driver usually drives the whole trip and stays at the same
  hotels (driver accommodation is normally provided by hotels).

## Never say

- **No prices** (driver, hotel, entrance fees, safaris) — say "voir sur le
  site" or "écrivez-nous" instead. Prices change and must come from the site.
- **No driver names, phone numbers or personal details.**
- **Trains:** the hill-country train (Kandy–Ella) is switched off in our
  trip planner because it may not be running normally. Don't build posts
  around it; if it comes up, say to check whether it's running, that seats
  sell out and that services stop after heavy rain — never promise it.
- No visa rules, health rules or safety claims stated as fact — those
  change; tell people to check official sources.
- No invented reviews, testimonials, client stories or statistics — and no
  invented first-hand moments ("ce matin-là, on était…"). A story post must
  be a template with [brackets] for the founder to fill in from something
  that really happened, e.g. "[le lieu], [ce qui s'est passé]".
- No claims of being the "best", "cheapest" or "n°1".

## Seasonal calendar hints

- Oct–Nov: people planning winter trips → seasons, itineraries, how many days.
- Dec–Jan: Christmas/New Year travel, south coast, whales.
- Feb–Apr: hill country, Sigiriya, Easter holidays (families).
- May–Sept: east coast, Arugam Bay, summer holidays (families, long trips).
- Sept–Oct: planning for next winter.
