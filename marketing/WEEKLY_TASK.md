# Weekly content pack — instructions

You prepare one week of social content for Voyagees. The founder reviews it
and posts manually.

1. Read `marketing/BRIEF.md` carefully.
2. Read the last 4 packs in `marketing/weekly/` (if any) so you don't repeat
   topics, openings or photo ideas. Also read `marketing/FEEDBACK.md` if it
   exists — it holds the founder's comments and overrides your defaults.
3. Work out the date of the coming Monday (the week the pack is for) and
   the season, using the calendar hints in the brief. If today is Monday,
   that's today. If a pack for that Monday already exists, prepare the
   first following week that has no pack yet — never overwrite an existing
   pack. (The founder sometimes posts ahead of the dates, so packs work as
   a queue.)
4. Write `marketing/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (that Monday's date) containing:

   ## Facebook group — 3 posts
   For each: suggested day (Mon/Wed/Fri or similar), the full post text in
   French ready to copy-paste, and a photo idea (what to shoot or pick from
   the founder's library). Mix types across the week: practical advice,
   a question/engagement post, a photo or story post. At most one of the
   three may mention Voyagees (trip planner, driver search or WhatsApp),
   softly, and with the right https://voyagees.com/fr link.

   ## Facebook Page + Instagram — 3 posts
   Matching the week's themes, posted together via Meta Business Suite:
   suggested day (offset from the group posts, e.g. Tue/Thu/Sat), the
   caption in French, then a separate line of 5–10 hashtags (for
   Instagram), and the photo/reel idea. One of the three may present a
   Voyagees service directly (trip planner, private driver, airport
   transfer) with its https://voyagees.com/fr link.

   ## Comment replies
   2–3 ready-made, friendly French replies to questions people commonly ask
   in Sri Lanka travel groups this time of year (useful for answering in
   other groups by hand — helpful first, Voyagees mentioned only if natural).

   ## Note for the founder
   2–3 lines in English: why these topics this week, and anything to check.

5. Check every post against the brief's "Never say" list before saving.
6. Commit the file to the `marketing-content` branch with a message like
   "Content pack for week of YYYY-MM-DD" and push. On this branch, only
   touch files inside `marketing/`.
7. Write this week's blog article by following `marketing/BLOG_TASK.md`
   (read it from this branch before switching to main). Choose the topic
   first, and in the pack's Page + Instagram section add one extra post
   sharing the article (link https://voyagees.com/fr/blog/<slug>), marked
   "post only once the article is live". Do steps 6 and 7 in that order:
   commit the pack, then write the article on its own branch.
8. Finish with a short summary in English: the week's topics, the pack
   filename, and the blog pull request link.
