**The best time to visit Sri Lanka is December to April for the south and west coasts and the hill country, and May to September for the east coast.** The Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa) works for most of the year. The island has two monsoons that hit different coasts at different times, so there's almost always somewhere with good weather. The easiest approach is to pick your dates first, then choose the route that suits them.

## At a glance

- **December to April**: the south and west coasts, and the hill country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Ella)
- **May to September**: the east coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay, Pasikudah)
- **Most of the year**: the Cultural Triangle (Sigiriya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa)
- **October and November**: the inter-monsoon, the wettest period across much of the island

These are trends, not guarantees. The weather can always surprise you, and a passing storm rarely spoils a trip.

## Where to go, month by month

### January

One of the best months for a first trip: calm seas on the south coast, clear skies in the hills and around the Cultural Triangle. It's peak whale-watching season off Mirissa. The Duruthu Perahera, a temple procession, takes place at Kelaniya near Colombo on the January full moon. Prices stay high until mid-January, after the holidays.

### February

Probably the easiest month of all: good weather almost everywhere, including the North. It's a great time to combine Sigiriya, Kandy, Ella and the beach. The Adam's Peak (Sri Pada) pilgrimage season is in full swing; people climb through the night to watch the sunrise from the summit.

### March

Still an excellent time for the south and the hills, with slightly fewer visitors than in January. It gets hotter in the Cultural Triangle, so visit Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa early in the morning. Whales are often still around off Mirissa.

### April

The last month of the season in the south and the first on the east coast. Sinhala and Tamil New Year, around 13 and 14 April, is a big family celebration: many Sri Lankans travel, and Nuwara Eliya has its own high season. Book early if you're heading to the hills. Late-afternoon thunderstorms are common.

### May

The south-west monsoon arrives: the south and west coasts become more changeable, while the east coast moves into its best season. Surf season starts in Arugam Bay. The Vesak full moon in May fills towns with lanterns and roadside stalls handing out free food. The Adam's Peak season ends at Vesak.

### June

The east coast, the Cultural Triangle and the North are the best choices. The south coast is rainy, but not every day: showers often alternate with sunny spells. The Poson full moon in June draws thousands of pilgrims to Anuradhapura and Mihintale.

### July

A good match for the summer holidays: Trincomalee, Pasikudah and Arugam Bay are in full season. Depending on the lunar calendar, Kandy's great Esala Perahera takes place in July or August: several nights of processions with elephants, dancers and drummers. Hotels in Kandy fill up fast on those dates.

### August

The ideal month to combine the Cultural Triangle with the east coast. Around August and September, when the dry season shrinks the water holes, hundreds of elephants gather around the Minneriya and Kaudulla reservoirs, one of the great wildlife sights in the country. The south coast and the hills are still possible, with changeable days.

### September

The end of the east coast season, with fewer people than in August. Yala National Park usually closes for a few weeks around September and October, so check the dates before planning a safari, or choose Udawalawe or Minneriya instead. It's often a good month for lower prices.

### October

The inter-monsoon: rain affects much of the island, mostly in the afternoons. Travel is still possible with flexible plans and short drives, and prices are lower. It's also an excellent time to book your winter trip.

### November

The east coast and the North move into their rainy season, while the south coast improves through the month. By late November, sunny days return to Mirissa and Galle. A good compromise if you'd like to avoid the December crowds and don't mind a few showers.

### December

The dry season returns to the south and west coasts and the hill country, along with the whales off Mirissa. The Adam's Peak season opens on the December full moon. Christmas and New Year are the busiest weeks of the year, so book hotels and your driver several weeks ahead.

## The south and west coasts: December to April

This is the season of calm seas and long beach days. If you're dreaming of [Mirissa](/destinations/mirissa), [Galle](/destinations/galle) or [Bentota](/destinations/bentota), this is the time. Whales can also be seen off Mirissa from roughly December to April, though sightings are never guaranteed.

It's also high season, especially around Christmas and New Year. If you're travelling then, book your accommodation early.

## The hill country: also December to April

Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Ella share the same season. Mornings and evenings are cool, especially up in [Nuwara Eliya](/destinations/nuwara-eliya), so bring a light jacket. During the day it's ideal for tea plantations and walks.

[Ella](/destinations/ella) is the kind of place where people end up staying longer than planned. Allow 5 to 6 hours by road from Kandy, and try not to arrive too late.

## The east coast: May to September

When the south coast is less pleasant, it's the east's turn. [Trincomalee](/destinations/trincomalee), [Pasikudah](/destinations/passikudah) and [Arugam Bay](/destinations/arugambay) are the places to be in summer. It's also surf season in Arugam Bay, from about May to September.

It's a good option if you're travelling in the summer holidays and want beach time: you don't have to wait for winter.

## The Cultural Triangle: most of the year

[Sigiriya](/destinations/sigiriya), Dambulla and [Polonnaruwa](/destinations/polonnaruwa) are pleasant for most of the year. It's hot, so visit early in the morning, before the heat and the crowds. That makes it the region that fits almost any travel dates.

> Not flexible on dates? Start from your travel period rather than a region. In summer, think east coast; in winter, think south coast and hills.

## Travelling with children: which season?

School holidays don't always line up neatly with the monsoons. For the Christmas holidays and February half-term, the south coast and the hills are good choices. For Easter, the hills and the Cultural Triangle work well. In the summer holidays, the east coast is the obvious pick for beaches.

With children, the main advice is to slow down: fewer stops, shorter drives, and a proper beach break in the middle or at the end. A five-hour drive is long in the back of a car, whatever the season.

## How long should you stay?

Whatever the season, roads are slow: plan on an average of 30 to 40 km an hour. Some reference times from the airport: about 4 hours to Sigiriya and about 3 hours to Kandy. Sigiriya to Mirissa takes 6 to 7 hours, so don't try it in a single day.

- **7 days**: one or two regions, for example Sigiriya and Kandy, or the south coast
- **10 to 12 days**: Sigiriya, Kandy, Ella and the south coast
- **15 days or more**: add a safari and/or the east coast, depending on the season

The principle is always the same: two nights in one place beats changing hotels every morning.

## Common mistakes

- **Mixing up the coasts**: planning the east coast in December or the south coast in July without checking the season
- **Cramming in too much**: trying to see the hills, the south coast and the North in a single week
- **Underestimating the roads**: judging journeys by distance on the map instead of driving time
- **Leaving it late**: the most popular hotels on the south coast fill up early around the holidays

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best month to visit Sri Lanka?

February and March are the easiest: the weather is good across most of the island, so you can combine culture, the hills and the south coast beaches. January is just as pleasant, but pricier because of the holidays.

### Can you visit Sri Lanka in July or August?

Yes. It's the season for the east coast (Trincomalee, Pasikudah, Arugam Bay) and a very good time for the Cultural Triangle and the elephants at Minneriya. It's best not to plan your beach week on the south coast.

### Is Sri Lanka hot all year round?

On the coasts and in the Cultural Triangle it's warm all year, around 30 °C during the day. The hill country is noticeably cooler, especially Nuwara Eliya, where evenings can be chilly, so pack a light jacket.

### Should you avoid October and November?

Not necessarily. There's more rain, mostly in the afternoons, but prices are lower and there are fewer visitors. With a flexible plan and short drives, it can still be a lovely trip.
